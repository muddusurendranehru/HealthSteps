# 🎨 SPEEDOMETER GAUGE FEATURE ADDED!

## ✅ What Was Added

A beautiful **circular speedometer gauge** that shows your daily steps progress with:

### 🎯 Color Zones (Like Real Speedometer):
- **🔴 RED (0-5,000 steps):** "Getting Started"
- **🟠 ORANGE (5,000-10,000 steps):** "Keep Going!"
- **🔵 BLUE (10,000-12,000 steps):** "Great Progress!"
- **🟢 GREEN (12,000+ steps):** "Excellent! 🎯"

### ✨ Features:
- **Moving Needle:** Animates smoothly as steps increase
- **Digital Display:** Shows exact step count
- **Status Badge:** Dynamic status text with color
- **Color-Coded Arc:** Progress arc changes color based on zone
- **Smooth Animations:** Needle and colors transition beautifully
